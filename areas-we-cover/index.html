Fire risk assessments across Scotland

We are based at 272 Bath Street in Glasgow and carry out fire risk assessments in every part of Scotland, from city-centre tenements to island guest houses.

In short: Fire Risk Scotland covers all 32 Scottish council areas from our Glasgow office, including Glasgow, Edinburgh, Aberdeen, Dundee, Inverness, Stirling, Perth, the Highlands and the islands. Every assessment is carried out in person by Raj Chall BSc, and prices start from £225 + VAT.

Glasgow and the west

Our office is in Glasgow city centre, so Glasgow, East and West Dunbartonshire, Renfrewshire, East Renfrewshire, Inverclyde and North and South Lanarkshire are covered with the shortest notice. Much of the work here is HMOs in tenement flats, city-centre offices and shops, and common closes for factors.

Edinburgh

We carry out assessments across the City of Edinburgh, including short-term lets, HMOs, guest houses, offices and the many listed buildings in the Old Town and New Town.

Fire risk assessments in Edinburgh

Ayrshire, Lanarkshire and Renfrewshire

North, South and East Ayrshire, including Ayr, Kilmarnock, Irvine and the coast, plus Paisley, Greenock, Hamilton, Motherwell, East Kilbride and Lanark. Coastal guest houses and holiday lets are common here, alongside industrial units and town-centre shops.

Fife and the Lothians

Fife, including Dunfermline, Kirkcaldy, Glenrothes and St Andrews, and West Lothian, Midlothian and East Lothian, including Livingston, Dalkeith, Musselburgh and North Berwick. St Andrews has a large stock of student HMOs and holiday lets.

Stirling, Falkirk and central Scotland

Stirling, Falkirk, Clackmannanshire and the surrounding towns. Work here includes tourist accommodation around the Trossachs and Loch Lomond, offices, schools and community buildings.

Dundee, Perth and Angus

Dundee, Perth and Kinross, and Angus, including Arbroath, Montrose, Forfar, Crieff and Pitlochry. Dundee's student population means a high number of HMOs, while Perthshire has many hotels, guest houses and holiday lets.

Aberdeen and the north-east

Aberdeen City, Aberdeenshire and Moray, including Peterhead, Fraserburgh, Inverurie, Stonehaven, Huntly and Elgin. Work includes offices, industrial premises, HMOs and rural tourist accommodation.

Inverness, Highlands and islands

Inverness and the Highland Council area, Argyll and Bute, the Western Isles, Orkney and Shetland. Guest houses, hotels, bunkhouses, self-catering lets and community halls make up much of the work. We plan Highland and island visits efficiently and can group several properties into one trip.

The Borders and Dumfries and Galloway

The Scottish Borders, including Galashiels, Hawick, Kelso and Peebles, and Dumfries and Galloway, including Dumfries, Stranraer, Castle Douglas and Kirkcudbright. Rural holiday lets, guest houses, farm buildings and town-centre premises are typical.

Frequently asked questions

Do you charge extra for travel to the Highlands and islands?

Remote and island locations can affect the price, and you will see the cost in your quote before you book. Where we have several properties to assess in the same area, we group visits to keep costs down.

Do you cover the north of England?

Fire Risk Scotland specialises in Scottish premises and Scottish fire law. Our sister company, Scottish Energy Services, works across Scotland and the north of England for energy assessments and building compliance.
