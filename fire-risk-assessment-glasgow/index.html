Glasgow's buildings and what they mean for fire safety

Much of Glasgow's housing and commercial space is in traditional sandstone tenements: four storeys or more, with a single common close and stair serving every flat. With only one way out, the condition of the stair, the flat entrance doors that open onto it, and anything stored in the close matter more than in almost any other type of building.

Ground-floor shops, bars and takeaways below residential flats are common across the city. In these mixed-use buildings, the business is a relevant premises under the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 and its fire risk assessment must consider the people living above, especially where the shop's kitchen or storage is next to the residential stair.

Glasgow City Council licenses HMOs and short-term lets in the city, and fire safety is part of both licensing schemes. If you are applying or renewing, tell us so the report covers what the council asks for.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a fire risk assessment in Glasgow?

From £225 + VAT for smaller premises such as an HMO flat, small office or shop. Larger premises, tenement common areas and hotels are quoted individually.

Can you assess an HMO in Glasgow before my licence renewal?

Yes. We recommend booking well ahead of the renewal date so there is time to complete any actions before the application goes in.

Do you assess tenement closes for factors in Glasgow?

Yes. We carry out common area fire risk assessments of tenement closes, stairs, back courts and bin stores for factors, owners and housing associations, following BS 9792:2025.
