How much does a fire risk assessment cost?

Prices start from £225 + VAT for smaller premises. Here is what affects the price, what is included, and how to get an accurate quote.

By Raj Chall BSc, IFE-registered fire risk assessor. Updated 12 November 2026.

In short: a fire risk assessment from Fire Risk Scotland costs from £225 + VAT for smaller premises such as a small office, shop, HMO flat or holiday let. Larger and more complex premises are quoted individually. Every quote covers the site visit, the written report and the action plan.

What affects the price

  • Size and layout

    Floor area, number of storeys and rooms, and how many stairs and escape routes there are.

  • Sleeping risk

    Premises where people sleep need more detailed assessment of detection, compartmentation and night-time evacuation.

  • Complexity and hazards

    Industrial processes, dangerous substances, commercial kitchens, or a mix of uses in one building.

  • Occupants

    The number of people and their needs, for example care home residents who need help to evacuate.

  • Location

    Travel to remote and island locations can affect the price.

  • Information available

    Floor plans and existing records make the assessment quicker, though neither is essential.

What is included

Every assessment includes:

  • Site visit

    A full in-person inspection by Raj Chall BSc, IFE-registered fire risk assessor.

  • Written report

    A premises-specific report with photographs, issued within 48 hours of the visit.

  • Risk rating and action plan

    An overall risk rating and every action ranked by priority with a timescale.

  • Questions answered

    Questions about the report answered by the assessor who wrote it.

Why the cheapest assessment can cost more

The Scottish Fire and Rescue Service warns that it can be difficult to judge the competence of companies and people advertising fire risk assessment services, and that the duty holder stays legally responsible whoever carries out the assessment. A low-cost, generic or desk-based assessment that misses a serious problem leaves you exposed to enforcement, licensing refusals and insurance disputes, and the work may have to be done again. A specific, competent assessment usually costs less overall because the action plan targets what actually matters.

How to get an accurate quote

  1. Tell us the address and type of premises

    For example, a five-bedroom HMO flat in Glasgow, or a three-storey office in Stirling.

  2. Give a rough size

    Number of floors and rooms, or an approximate floor area.

  3. Say why you need it

    A licence application, an insurer's request, an SFRS visit, or a routine review.

  4. Receive your quote

    We confirm the price and book a visit date that suits you.

Frequently asked questions

How much is a fire risk assessment for a small business in Scotland?

From £225 + VAT with Fire Risk Scotland for smaller premises such as a small office, shop or unit. The price includes the written report and action plan.

How much is an HMO fire risk assessment?

From £225 + VAT for a typical HMO flat or small house. Larger HMOs, and buildings containing several HMOs, are quoted individually.

Do you charge for travel?

We cover all of Scotland, including the Highlands and islands. Remote and island locations can affect the price, and your quote will show this before you book.

Do you charge for a review of an existing assessment?

A review is quoted in the same way. If the premises have changed significantly, a new assessment may be more appropriate, and we will tell you which you need.

Do you offer discounts for several properties?

Landlords, letting agents, factors and businesses with several premises can ask for a single quote covering the whole portfolio, with visits scheduled efficiently.
