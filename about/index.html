About Fire Risk Scotland

A specialist fire risk assessment company, set up to do one thing well: fire risk assessments for Scottish premises, written to Scottish law, by an assessor you can check.

Raj Chall BSc, lead fire risk assessor

Raj has worked in the fire safety industry for 15 years. He holds a BSc and is listed on the Institution of Fire Engineers (IFE) fire risk assessor register, one of the professional registers the Scottish Fire and Rescue Service lists for duty holders choosing an assessor.

  • BSc
  • IFE fire risk assessor register
  • 15 years in fire safety

Raj carries out every Fire Risk Scotland assessment in person and writes every report himself. The person who walks your building, opens the plant room and talks to your staff is the person who decides the risk rating and writes the action plan. If you have a question about the report, you speak to him.

How we work

  • Scottish law, not borrowed English templates

    Every report is written to the Fire (Scotland) Act 2005 and the Fire Safety (Scotland) Regulations 2006, using Scottish Government guidance.

  • Specific to your premises

    No generic reports. The assessment describes your building, your occupants and your risks.

  • Proportionate actions

    Actions are ranked by priority, explained in plain English, and sized to the actual risk, not to what a contractor might want to sell.

  • Fixed fees

    From £225 + VAT, agreed before the visit, with travel included.

  • Fast reports

    Your written report within 48 hours of the site visit, and urgent issues raised on the day.

  • Honest about scope

    We tell you what an assessment covers and what it does not, and when you need a different specialist.

Part of Scottish Energy Services

Fire Risk Scotland is a sister company of Scottish Energy Services, the Glasgow energy and building compliance consultancy at 272 Bath Street. Scottish Energy Services provides commercial and domestic EPCs, display energy certificates, SAP and SBEM calculations, Section 63 assessments, TM59 overheating assessments, dynamic simulation modelling and legionella risk assessments, across Scotland and the north of England.

Fire Risk Scotland was set up as a dedicated specialist for fire risk assessment, so that landlords, businesses and factors looking for fire safety find a company focused entirely on it. If the same property needs an EPC, a legionella risk assessment or energy calculations, both companies can work together to arrange it with one visit and one point of contact.
